# Editorial campaign imagery

The storefront hero and brand landing pages currently use temporary editorial images from Unsplash rather than abstract SVG placeholders. They are reference imagery for layout and art direction, not a claim that these are VÉRANE's own campaigns.

## Current sources

- **UTHY LUXURY** — [Young woman in a white suit](https://unsplash.com/photos/young-woman-in-white-suit-posing-against-white-background-7uDwMv9FlrQ), photographed by Shane Ryan Herilalaina. The Unsplash page identifies this image as free to use under the Unsplash License.
- **ALOMZIEE FOOTIES** — [Two young women in streetwear](https://unsplash.com/photos/two-young-women-posing-in-casual-streetwear-outfits-VJAXXDs3UdU), photographed by Ben Iwara in Lagos, Nigeria. The Unsplash page identifies this image as free to use under the Unsplash License.

The image component requests responsive-format JPEG/AVIF/WebP delivery from Unsplash's image CDN at a 1600px maximum width and quality 78. Images are loaded eagerly only for the hero and lazily elsewhere.

## Before final production launch

Replace these temporary images with VÉRANE-owned or explicitly approved campaign photography, uploaded to the configured media storage and selected through the content editor. Keep the image composition and brand contrast, but do not present stock photography as original brand campaign work. Product images should continue to come from each product's media records; this fallback does not replace catalog photography.
